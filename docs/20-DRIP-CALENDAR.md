## §1 Standing rules

- **The freeze:** everything intended for Sep–Oct is written and locked by Aug 31; from ~mid-Oct
  through the midterms, no new backward-looking claims, live-product observation and pre-locked
  pieces only (docs/06 election-freeze discipline applied to editorial).
- **Reversal-direction claims need re-registration** (S1.2 peaked-and-fell, S2.7 pronoun,
  Sunday-as-positive-claim, etc.): registered in October, tested **out-of-sample on the CREC lane**
  after SD.8 calibration, the twice-seen-once-registered pieces land spring 2027. Adjudicated
  nulls/artifacts (S4.4, S1.10) need no re-registration; the adjudication is the piece.
- **Throughput is the constraint, not inventory.** Assume ~2 Michael-hours/month for editorial
  approval; Opus drafts from cards, Michael edits and publishes (operator-is-not-the-instrument,
  voice and byline are his). Cadence target 1–2/month; the calendar below averages ~2.
- Every piece: receipts, symmetric framing, correlation-not-cause labels where applicable; anything
   goes through neutrality review before lock.

## §3 The capacity math, said plainly

Nine named pieces through December + a 12-part series + per-case season + ~8 feature-release moments
≈ **20+ content moments over 12 months without a single new confirmed finding.** Everything new that
lands (S1.12, S5.2, S3.7, SD.2, the CREC reversal tests, the live 2026 cycle itself) is upside, not
critical. The scarce resource is Michael's editorial hours and the credibility budget, spend
both slowly. The midterm plan is deliberate: **findings before November build credibility; features
during November catch the spike.**
