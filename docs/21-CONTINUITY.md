# 21. Continuity without fable (Fable, 2026-07-18, binding until superseded)

Written because Fable may become unavailable as a model. This is the standing answer to "who plays
Fable's role now." Read it whenever a session needs a planning decision, a new brief, a ruling, or a
publication review and no Fable session can be had. Companion continuity docs: `01-VISION.md` +
`05-HORIZON.md` (the idea reservoir, future planning draws from these instead of re-ideating),
`06-CONSTITUTION.md` (the invariants), this doc (the process).

## §1 What already stands and needs no Fable

Every binding artifact executes without me. The governing chain, in the order a fresh session
encounters it: **claude.md** (state + rulings + build order) → the active brief (**docs/19** now) →
the standing programs (**docs/11** build, **docs/12** search laws, **docs/15** deep archive,
**docs/16** nomenclature spec, **docs/17/18** re-validation canon) → the operating plans
(**docs/20**
drip calendar, **docs/07** operations). The Session-13c rulings R1–R6, the Intensification ruling
(#174, Michael-confirmed), and Article xvi are all in force as written. **Implementing sessions
execute; they never re-litigate.** None of this requires Fable to exist.

## §2 The role split when Fable is absent

Fable never held final authority. **Michael did**; Fable drafted and Michael confirmed. So the seat
does not go empty, it just loses its drafter. The replacement protocol:

- **Opus drafts, Michael rules.** When a genuinely new decision arises (not covered by a standing
  ruling), Opus writes a short decision memo, the options, the evidence for each, a recommendation,
  and what each option changes downstream, files a vtask, and stops. The memo style to imitate is
  the #143/#146 adjudications and the Intensification ruling.
- **Opus never self-authorizes the reserved list**, exactly as before: flag flips · releases ·
  repo-public · posting · publication of any card or article · the docs/16 §9 rulings · constitution
  amendments · anything that changes the neutrality posture or a published number's meaning. A
  reserved decision made "to keep moving" is a defect even when it is correct.
- **Draft briefs are legal but not binding.** Opus may author a new brief in the docs/17–19 format
  when the queue needs one, headed "DRAFT. Michael to ratify." It binds only after Michael says so
  in-session; the ratification is recorded in claude.md like #174.

4. **New defect classes / external critiques**: the Session-20 protocol, reproduce from the real
   artifacts first; classify against the reserved list; if reserved, decision memo + vtask; if not,
   fix with kill-fixtures both directions and log it. Never argue with a critique from memory.

## §4 If a planning model returns

A future Fable (or successor planning model) inherits by reading `01-VISION.md`, `05-HORIZON.md`,
`06-CONSTITUTION.md`, the claude.md rulings, and this doc, then resumes the drafter's seat. Ratified
interim rulings made under §2 stand; a successor may propose amendments but nothing is silently
reversed (append-only, like the ledger).
